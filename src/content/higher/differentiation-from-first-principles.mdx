# Differentiation from first principles

Higher learners should understand that a derivative is a limit of average rate of change.

## Why this matters

The derivative measures how a quantity changes at a point.

## The idea

We begin with the difference quotient:

$$
\frac{f(x+h)-f(x)}{h}
$$

As $h$ becomes very small, this approaches the gradient of the tangent.

## Worked example

For $f(x)=x^2$:

$$
\frac{(x+h)^2-x^2}{h} = \frac{2xh+h^2}{h}=2x+h
$$

As $h\to 0$, the derivative is $2x$.

## Common mistake

A derivative is not the same as the average slope over an interval. It is the slope at a single point.
