# Algebraic manipulation

Algebra gives us a way to describe relationships and solve problems without writing every number explicitly.

## Why this matters

Manipulation is the bridge between a statement and a usable solution.

## The idea

A variable is a symbol that stands for a quantity. Expressions are built from variables, numbers and operations.

## Worked example

Expand and simplify:

$$
3(x+2)=3x+6
$$

## Common mistake

Do not forget that multiplication is distributed across every term inside the bracket.
