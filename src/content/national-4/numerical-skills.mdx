# Numerical skills and calculations

National 4 learners should be able to use numerical methods confidently in familiar contexts.

## Why this matters

Numerical fluency helps with money, measurement, estimation and data.

## The idea

A strong grasp of place value, fractions, decimals and percentages makes later algebra and problem solving easier.

## Worked example

To find $15\%$ of $240$:

$$
0.15 \times 240 = 36
$$

So the answer is $36$.

## Common mistake

Percent means "out of 100". A shift from $15\%$ to $0.15$ is a change of form, not a change of meaning.
